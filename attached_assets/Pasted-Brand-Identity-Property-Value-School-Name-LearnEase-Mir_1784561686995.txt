Key Design Decisions
Single-page scroll — Reduces friction for parents browsing on mobile. Everything is one scroll away.
Blue + Teal palette — Blue = trust/stability (critical for a school), Teal = growth/freshness. Both strongly associated with education.
Plus Jakarta Sans — More distinctive than Inter, warm yet modern, highly readable at all sizes.
Yellow in hero — School name pops against the blue overlay without being garish — creates a memorable visual anchor.
Dark navy footer — Strong visual closure; feels premium against the light body sections.
useInView animations — Fires only when element enters viewport, so users feel the content responds to them rather than auto-playing on load.
Gallery lightbox — Lets parents/students view school life photos in detail without leaving the page, increasing engagement time.
Inline forms — Keeping admissions and contact forms on-page (no redirects) reduces drop-off significantly.
